# Attribution

PowerUI is MIT licensed. It began from Hallmark, the original design skill by Nutlope and contributors, and keeps Hallmark's copyright and permission notice in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

## Inherited Hallmark material

The following material is inherited from Hallmark and remains under the MIT license:

- The 45 catalog themes and their pictures in `powerui/references/themes/` (every theme except the five original PowerUI themes listed below).
- The four inherited proof pages: `examples/professional/`, `examples/kids/`, `examples/b2b/`, `examples/studio/`, plus `examples/proofs-grid.png`.
- The reference library and scripts that predate PowerUI, including the shared flow, layout, craft, gate and theme tools.

## Original PowerUI material

The following material is new PowerUI work:

- The five original themes and pictures: `powerui/references/themes/receiver.md` and `receiver.jpg`; `tidepool.md` and `tidepool.jpg`; `foundry.md` and `foundry.jpg`; `parcel.md` and `parcel.jpg`; `sundial.md` and `sundial.jpg`; and their entries in `powerui/references/themes/index.json`.
- The five original proof pages: `examples/radio/`, `examples/tidepool/`, `examples/foundry/`, `examples/parcel/` and `examples/sundial/`.
- The original screenshots and grid: `examples/receiver.png` and `examples/original-pages-grid.png`.
- The automatic project-fit workflow: `powerui/references/automatic.md`, `powerui/scripts/fit.mjs`, `powerui/scripts/fit.test.mjs`.
- The proof report workflow: `powerui/scripts/proof.mjs`, `powerui/scripts/proof.test.mjs`.
- The documentation that describes these additions, including the READMEs and this file.

The five original themes are original materials, not renamed Hallmark themes. The four new script and test files are the fit/proof pair and their tests. The other scripts in `powerui/scripts/` were present in the Hallmark import; PowerUI has renamed and adjusted some of them for its package and version, but they are not new PowerUI tools.
