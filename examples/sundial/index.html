The residency.

Sundial is a small residency for artists who need a room and time.

The house keeps three studios, a shared kitchen and a garden. Artists live on site, work at their own pace and leave one door open on Sundays.

No programme of talks. The season is the programme.

Three rooms.

Each studio has north light, a sink and a door to the garden.
