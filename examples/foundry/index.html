From pattern to pour.

Four moves, in the same order.

  1. 01

    Pattern

    A positive in wood or foam.

    Tolerance
    2 mm

  2. 02

    Mould

    Sand packed around the pattern.

    Box
    450 mm

  3. 03

    Pour

    Metal heated until it runs.

    Iron
    1,180 C

  4. 04

    Finish

    Cut, grind and patina.

    Cool
    24 h
